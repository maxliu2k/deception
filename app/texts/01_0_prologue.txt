Prologue

“It is a complete understatement to say that the kingdom has been blessed under our King; in truth, His Majesty may be the greatest ruler the world has seen in several centuries. It is therefore most unfortunate that rumors of his failing health grow louder by the day, and I would be lying if I claimed they do not unsettle me. What troubles me most is Arthur. He is my closest friend, which is perhaps why I know better than anybody what could happen to our kingdom once he gets his hands on the crown. His ambition is simply never-ending, and once he fixes his will upon the expansion of our kingdom, he will stop at no ends, for the better or worse of its inhabitants. I do not believe he sees, or cares for, the cost of his visions. I do not know what he will force me to do as Chancellor, and some nights I fear that his hunger for conquest may be my own undoing. I pray these thoughts are born of exhaustion rather than foresight, but sleep comes poorly all the same.”
-excerpt from a journal entry written by Lord Charles Capet, dated September 19th, 3123



“It is with great sorrow that the Kingdom announces the passing of His Majesty King Arthur IX the Great, who died peacefully upon his throne at the age of seventy-three. His reign was marked by strength and generosity; under his rule, the Kingdom expanded its borders and enacted policies intended to better the lives of its people. His Majesty is survived by his son, Prince Arthur, who shall ascend the throne as King Arthur X. The coronation ceremony is to be held on August 21st at the King’s Palace, and citizens from every town are invited to attend. Further details will be announced in the coming days. The Kingdom mourns the loss of its King and honors his memory. Long live the new King.”
-Chronicle of the Crown, daily issue on June 5th, 3125
