Collided Worlds

“Legend says that our world was once many worlds, each with its own people and its own history, until they were drawn together by the overwhelming power of Creation, and I find no reason to dismiss these stories outright. Tellura itself offers quiet evidence: its climates, geology, flora and fauna have developed in ways that hint at more than a single beginning. For generations, scholars have returned to this mystery, hoping to understand why Creation shaped the world as it did. Many devoted their lives to the question, and many more have lost themselves to madness in its pursuit. This small section of the library is my own attempt to record what can be known with care and honesty. Much remains uncertain, and some answers may truly never be discovered. Keep that in mind as you read: there are limits to what the past will reveal, and that is why you must never forget the present.”
-inscription by Albus Alzaren, Grand Scholar, dated January 1st, 3000, above the Divine Library



“Our current estimates place the Overlord’s return somewhere around the year 3136—give or take a margin I would rather not argue about in public. The evidence also suggests that it may be stronger than ever, and therefore neglecting our defenses would be especially unwise. I therefore encourage the world’s leaders to prepare accordingly. That said, panic has never improved anyone’s odds (although, to be blunt, ‘winging it’ never has either). We are, thankfully, past the era of practically useless Overseers, a fact that I (and I hope many of you) appreciate sincerely. I have complete confidence in the Overseer’s ability to defeat the dark spirit and perhaps seal it away permanently. However matters conclude, I suspect our historians will have quite a busy year.”
-formal announcement by Albus Alzaren, Grand Scholar, dated May 9th, 3128
